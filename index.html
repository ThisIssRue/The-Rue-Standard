About

Meet Rue

Ruvimbo Karimazondo, known as Rue, is a speaker, etiquette coach and entrepreneur with a background in diplomacy, leadership and international relations. Through The Rue Standard, she helps professionals, teams and brands carry themselves with confidence in every room that matters.

Rue’s conviction is simple: etiquette isn’t just good manners. It shapes how relationships are built, how opportunities are won and how leaders are remembered. Through her LinkedIn series, Business Etiquette with Rue, she shares that knowledge with professionals everywhere, and has become a respected voice on presence, communication and professional conduct.

Rue is also the Founder and CEO of Bénie, home to Bénie Events Management and Bénie Liquor Supplies, and was recognised at the Business Excellence Awards Zimbabwe as Most Innovative Director in Business Operations.

Rue brings an international outlook, a calm authority and a belief that excellence should be the minimum, not the exception.

“Your professional presence speaks before you do.”Rue
