About

Meet Rue

Ruvimbo Karimazondo, known as Rue, is a Head of Protocol whose work sits at the meeting point of diplomacy, leadership and international relations. She manages high-level engagements, guides dignitaries and executives, and keeps the confidence that her role demands.

Rue’s conviction is simple: protocol isn’t just ceremony. It shapes how trade deals are struck, how economies are represented and how leaders are remembered. Through her LinkedIn series, Business Etiquette with Rue, she shares that knowledge with professionals everywhere, and has become a respected voice on presence, communication and professional conduct.

Beyond protocol, Rue is the Founder and CEO of Bénie, home to Bénie Events Management and Bénie Liquor Supplies, and was recognised at the Business Excellence Awards Zimbabwe as Most Innovative Director in Business Operations.

Rue brings an international outlook, a calm authority and a belief that excellence should be the minimum, not the exception.

“Your professional presence speaks before you do.”Rue
