Privacy Notice

Your information, handled with discretion.

Last updated: November 2026

Discretion is central to The Rue Standard. This notice explains what information we collect when you make an enquiry, why we collect it and how we protect it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you choose to share:

  • your name, organisation, email address and phone number;
  • the topic you are enquiring about, and your event date, location, format and audience size;
  • anything you include in your message.

We do not use advertising trackers on this website.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry and to follow up on it. We will not sell, rent or share it for marketing.

Who we share it with

We do not share your information with anyone, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records only for as long as needed to respond and follow up, then delete them securely.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. To make a request, email bookings@theruestandard.com. We will respond promptly.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent to bookings@theruestandard.com.
