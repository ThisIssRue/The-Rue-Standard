Biography

Ruvimbo Karimazondo, known as Rue, is a Head of Protocol whose work sits at the meeting point of diplomacy, leadership and international relations. She manages high-level engagements, guides dignitaries and executives, and keeps the confidence that her role demands.

Through her LinkedIn series, Business Etiquette with Rue, she has become a respected voice on presence, communication and professional conduct.

Rue’s conviction is simple: protocol isn’t just ceremony. It shapes how trade deals are struck, how economies are represented and how leaders are remembered.

She is also the Founder and CEO of Bénie, home to Bénie Events Management and Bénie Liquor Supplies.

“Your professional presence speaks before you do.”Rue
